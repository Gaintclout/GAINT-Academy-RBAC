# Role Audit and Permission Matrix

## Institution Admin
Institution-wide control. Full administrative scope, user/role management, reporting and authorized safety monitoring.

## Teacher
Assigned academic scope. Can take attendance, create/update homework/assignments/exams/results and communicate. No finance/HR/admin/live-GPS administration.

## Student
Own academic scope. Attendance/homework/results are read-only. Can pay fees, raise own grievance and see own safety state. No other-student or admin access.

## Parent / Guardian
Linked-child scope. Attendance/homework/results are read-only. Can pay fees, message, raise grievance, request child leave and view linked-child live location only.

## Accounts
Finance-only operational scope: fees, payments, receipts, concessions, refunds, reconciliation and finance reports. No student GPS, HR or academic editing.

## HR
Staff lifecycle scope: staff, staff attendance, leave, documents, recruitment, performance and HR reports. No student GPS or finance administration.

## Campus Admin
Campus-scoped operations: students/staff view/update, campus attendance, transport, visitors, inventory/assets, events, grievances, reports and authorized live safety map.

## Auditor
Read-only assurance: audit trail, compliance, exceptions, evidence and export. No operational create/update/delete and no routine live student GPS.
